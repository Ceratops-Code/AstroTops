Kenney CC0 Assets
=================

AstroTops includes selected ship graphics from "Space Shooter Remastered" and
selected audio from "Sci-Fi Sounds" and "Interface Sounds", created and
distributed by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/).

These assets are dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero
(CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Milky Way Background
====================

`assets/milky_way_background.png` was generated specifically for AstroTops with
OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-24, using a project-owner-
supplied image only as a composition and color-mood reference. No third-party
source image is bundled as the game background.

Traxy Sprite Atlas
==================

`assets/traxy.png` was generated specifically for AstroTops with OpenAI's
built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-26, then assembled and resized into
the six-frame production atlas used by the game. No third-party source image is
bundled in the atlas.
